# Code of Conduct

OpenLens aims to be a calm, respectful, high-signal project for contributors and users.

## Expected Behavior

- be respectful, specific, and constructive
- assume good intent and discuss ideas on their merits
- keep feedback actionable and technically grounded
- protect user privacy and handle sensitive details carefully

## Unacceptable Behavior

- harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks
- trolling, baiting, or bad-faith arguments
- doxxing or publishing private information without consent
- posting credentials, exploit details, or sensitive user data in public threads

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject content that harms the project or its contributors. Repeated or severe violations may lead to temporary or permanent bans from project spaces.

For security issues, use the process in `SECURITY.md`.
