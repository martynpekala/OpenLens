Leave the desk, not the session.

OpenLens keeps the important parts of your OpenCode workflow close without pretending your phone is a tiny laptop.

  • Native session chat

    Markdown and code blocks, agent activity, permission prompts, and questions, all in a native iPhone and iPad interface.

  • Review changes

    Inspect diffs for a whole session or a single agent update, and revert one update without losing the rest.

  • Simple connection

    Scan a QR code, discover your Mac with Bonjour, or enter its address manually.

  • Remote Access

    Reach your Mac away from home through your own Cloudflare Tunnel, end-to-end encrypted, with no VPN.

  • Workspace

    Browse files, worktrees, and slash commands, and request branch switches, pushes, and pull requests.

  • Live Activities

    Follow agent progress on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island.
